AstraZeneca

Critical Evaluation of the Global Business Landscape

An independent academic case study examining how technological, political and economic changes influence the global pharmaceutical industry.

Ulster University | MSc International Business with HRM

Project Overview

This academic research evaluated the impact of changes in the global business landscape on AstraZeneca's strategic decisions and business operations, with a focus on the conditions prevailing in 2023.

Key Areas of Analysis

Technology

Explored artificial intelligence, digital healthcare, telemedicine and data analytics as opportunities for pharmaceutical innovation.

Political Environment

Examined intellectual property rights, international trade tensions, regulatory requirements and geopolitical developments.

Economic Environment

Evaluated economic uncertainty, healthcare spending, market demand and their implications for business growth.

Business Opportunities & Threats

Considered opportunities from healthcare innovation and partnerships alongside cybersecurity, regulatory and financial risks.

Key Research Insights

The analysis highlighted the importance of strategic adaptability in the global pharmaceutical industry.

Digital transformation, healthcare investment and international collaboration presented opportunities, while economic uncertainty, changing regulations and data security requirements created additional challenges.

Reflective Learning

As part of the Global Business in Context module, I also participated in a group presentation applying CAGE analysis to AstraZeneca in the UK and India.

My contribution focused on researching the economic dimension and collaborating with group members to integrate findings. The experience strengthened my teamwork, communication and research skills.
